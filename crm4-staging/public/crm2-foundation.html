Production-safety controls

What This Release Deliberately Does Not Do

  • It does not rename, redesign or refactor the existing CRM.
  • It does not display internet-generated leads inside CRM2.
  • It does not write CRM2 drafts into live CRM collections.
  • It does not claim final high-fidelity DOCX or PDF generation.
  • It does not include partner, office or third-party names in routes or UI.
