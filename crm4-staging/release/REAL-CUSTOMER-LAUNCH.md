## Optional improvements (not substitutes for mandatory controls)

Custom domain/branding, integrated telephony when an approved manual calling process works, AI assistance, additional assessment categories, larger scaling limits, richer dashboards and automated recovery drills beyond the initial proven restore. Live email/payments become mandatory only if the approved minimum business workflow actually depends on them. The GBP20 budget is an alert, not a spend cap; reliable billing and operational alert delivery must have an accountable recipient, while changing its specific contact/threshold requires the previously reserved exact approval.

No real-customer launch date can be certified until the security, synthetic-only code, identity, operational-role, privacy and recovery blockers above are closed. Parallel preparation can shorten the path; bypassing those gates cannot.
