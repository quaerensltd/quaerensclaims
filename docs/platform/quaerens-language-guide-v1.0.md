# The Quaerens Language Guide™

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved Platform Language Standard

## Purpose

The Quaerens Language Guide™ defines how The Quaerens Platform™ speaks.

Language is not decoration.

Language is part of the platform experience.

People should recognise Quaerens not only by its logo, products, systems or documents, but by the way it explains, reassures and guides.

The words used across Quaerens should create:

Understanding  
↓  
Confidence  
↓  
Trust  
↓  
Action

Language is therefore part of the platform itself.

Every public page, Complaint Pack, QCMS journey, operational message, client communication, partner document and future intelligence feature should follow this standard.

## The Quaerens Voice

The Quaerens voice should always sound:

- Warm
- Professional
- Calm
- Human
- Respectful
- Logical
- Confident
- Evidence-led
- Helpful
- Encouraging

The Quaerens voice should never sound:

- Aggressive
- Pushy
- Sales-driven
- Fear-based
- Sensational
- Complicated
- Patronising
- Overly legal

Quaerens should speak with quiet confidence.

It should not shout for attention.

It should help people feel that a confusing situation can be understood, organised and approached one step at a time.

## The Golden Rule

We explain.

We never overwhelm.

We guide.

We never pressure.

We educate.

We never exaggerate.

We build confidence.

We never create fear.

This rule applies to every page, form, message, builder, document, button, notice and support journey created within The Quaerens Platform™.

## Empathy First

Every public page should begin by acknowledging the person's situation.

People rarely arrive at Quaerens feeling certain. They may feel dismissed, confused, anxious, frustrated, embarrassed or unsure what matters.

The first responsibility of the page is to meet that person where they are.

Examples:

Flight:

"Flight disruption is frustrating enough without having to become an expert in passenger rights."

Spray Foam:

"Discovering that your home may now be harder to mortgage or sell can feel overwhelming."

Section 75:

"When a purchase goes wrong, understanding your rights shouldn't be the hardest part."

Holiday Park:

"Buying a holiday home should create memories, not uncertainty."

Car Finance:

"You shouldn't need a legal background to understand whether your finance agreement was fair."

Every new Complaint Centre should begin with empathy before education.

## Write For People

Never write for search engines first.

Never write for lawyers first.

Write for someone worried, uncertain and looking for clarity.

Assume intelligence.

Never assume specialist knowledge.

The reader should feel respected, not instructed from above.

The page should make the subject easier to understand without making the person feel that they should already know the answer.

## Common Language

The platform should use simple, consistent words that support understanding.

Use:

- People
- Platform Users
- Clients, only after QCMS instruction
- Complaint Managers
- Knowledge
- Evidence
- Confidence
- Choice
- Next Step
- Journey
- Guidance
- Support

Avoid:

- Claim now
- Act immediately
- Don't miss out
- Huge compensation
- Guaranteed
- Easy money
- Win
- Pressure language

Language should create informed confidence, not urgency for its own sake.

## Language Of Reassurance

Quaerens should help people understand their position without making promises that cannot be guaranteed.

Use calmer, clearer phrasing:

Instead of "You must..." prefer "You may wish to..."

Instead of "Claim compensation" prefer "Understand your options."

Instead of "Missing evidence" prefer "One additional document could strengthen your position."

Instead of "Complaint rejected" prefer "The business has not accepted your complaint at this stage."

Always explain what happens next.

Reassurance does not mean weakening the message.

It means making the next step feel visible, practical and proportionate.

## Questions Every Page Should Answer

Every public page should help the reader answer:

- What has happened?
- Why does it matter?
- What evidence helps?
- What are my options?
- What happens next?
- When might professional support help?

If a page does not answer these questions, the reader may leave with more uncertainty than they arrived with.

That is not acceptable for The Quaerens Platform™.

## Public Language And Internal Language

Public language should increasingly use Your Journey™ instead of overly technical workflow language.

People do not need to understand internal product architecture to understand what to do next.

Internal operational terminology can remain where it supports staff clarity:

- Complaint Journey™
- Complaint Readiness™
- Operations Centre™
- Case Workspace™

Public language should translate those concepts into plain, reassuring guidance.

The user should feel guided through a journey, not moved through a system.

## The Quaerens Promise

Whether you continue independently or ask us to help...

Our goal remains exactly the same.

To leave you with greater knowledge, stronger evidence and more confidence than when you arrived.

This promise should shape the tone of every sentence.

The platform may guide, assess, prepare, explain, organise and support.

It must never make a person feel that professional support is the only valid route unless that is genuinely the case.

## The Heart Of Language

We don't build software.

We remove uncertainty.

Every sentence should reduce uncertainty.

Every heading should orient the reader.

Every button should make the next step clear.

Every explanation should make the issue easier to understand.

Every warning should protect the reader without frightening them.

Every reassurance should be truthful.

Every page should leave the person better informed than before.

## The Language Test

Before publishing any page, ask:

- Does this acknowledge the person's situation?
- Does this reduce uncertainty?
- Does this build confidence?
- Does this respect the reader?
- Does this explain the next step?
- Does this educate before selling?

If not, rewrite it.

This test applies to public pages, Complaint Pack builders, QCMS journeys, operational messages, client communications, partner materials and future intelligence outputs.

## Conclusion

The Quaerens voice becomes recognisable because it consistently explains, guides, reassures, supports, educates and builds confidence.

It should never become recognisable because it shouts louder than competitors.

The strongest Quaerens language is calm, useful and clear.

It helps people understand what has happened, what evidence may help, what choices are available and what the next step could be.

It gives people confidence without promising certainty.

It creates trust by increasing understanding.

Evidence.

Clarity.

Results.
