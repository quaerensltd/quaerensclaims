# ADR-0001: The Birth of The Quaerens Platform™

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved

## Context

Quaerens began by developing individual products, pages, tools, operational workflows and service concepts.

Each product solved a real problem. Complaint Packs™ helped users prepare documents. Knowledge pages helped users understand their position. QCMS™ created a route into managed support. QCMS Operations™ began defining how instructed cases could be managed with discipline and clarity.

Over time, it became clear that these were not separate projects.

They were connected parts of one larger system.

## Decision

Quaerens formally became The Quaerens Platform™.

The company stopped treating its products as isolated initiatives and recognised them as one connected platform for consumer complaint resolution.

## Reasoning

Consumer complaints are rarely solved by one page, one letter or one interaction.

They involve knowledge, evidence, timelines, documents, decisions, escalation routes, communication and operational follow-through.

Treating each product separately would create duplication, inconsistency and fragmentation. Treating them as one platform creates shared language, shared standards and a clearer route for users and staff.

The platform model allows each product to have a distinct role while still contributing to one coherent journey.

## Long-Term Benefits

This decision creates:

- A unified strategic direction
- Clear separation between public tools, managed services and operations
- Better consistency across products
- A stronger foundation for future portals
- A reusable architecture for new complaint categories
- A clearer identity for users, staff and partners

The Quaerens Platform™ is now the organising idea behind all future development.
