# ADR-0003: Evidence-First Platform

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved

## Context

Many complaint journeys begin with frustration.

A user knows something has gone wrong, but may not yet know what evidence matters, what route is available or what outcome is realistic.

If a platform begins with a complaint before understanding the evidence, it risks creating weak, incomplete or misleading complaint materials.

## Decision

Quaerens will operate as an evidence-first platform.

The preferred journey is:

Knowledge  
↓  
Evidence  
↓  
Complaint

It is not:

Complaint  
↓  
Evidence

## Reasoning

A strong complaint depends on facts, documents, dates, correspondence, proof of loss and a clear timeline.

Starting with knowledge helps users understand the issue.

Starting with evidence helps the platform identify what is supported, what is missing and what route may be appropriate.

Only then should the complaint be structured.

This order protects users from overclaiming, underexplaining or sending complaint letters that do not match the available evidence.

## Long-Term Impact

Every future Quaerens feature should respect the evidence-first model.

This influences:

- Complaint Pack™ builders
- QCMS™ instruction journeys
- QCMS Operations™ case reviews
- Evidence Intelligence™
- Document Intelligence™
- Complaint Readiness™
- Smart Submission™

The platform should always help users understand what they can evidence before helping them decide what to say.
