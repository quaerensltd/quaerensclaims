# ADR-0005: The Quaerens Philosophy

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved

## Context

As Quaerens became a platform, it needed a governing philosophy.

The company required a principle that could guide product design, operational workflows, public content, managed services and future intelligence features.

That philosophy had to be simple enough to remember and strong enough to shape future decisions.

## Decision

Quaerens permanently records the following philosophy:

Every complaint begins with knowledge,
is strengthened by evidence,
guided by technology,
supported by people
and continually improves the platform
for everyone who follows.

## Reasoning

This philosophy reflects the full complaint journey.

Knowledge helps users understand what has happened.

Evidence gives the complaint strength.

Technology helps structure the process.

People provide judgement, support and care where the situation requires it.

Learning from the journey improves the platform for future users.

## Governing Principle

This philosophy became the governing principle of The Quaerens Platform™.

It should influence:

- Public guidance
- Complaint Packs™
- QCMS™
- QCMS Operations™
- Client Portal™
- Partner Portal™
- Evidence Intelligence™
- Document Intelligence™
- Future product decisions

## Long-Term Impact

The philosophy protects Quaerens from building technology for its own sake.

Every feature must serve the complaint journey.

Every workflow must help users or staff move with more confidence.

Every future innovation must remain grounded in knowledge, evidence, technology, people and continuous improvement.
