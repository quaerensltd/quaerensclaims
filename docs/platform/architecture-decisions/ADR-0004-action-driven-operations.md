# ADR-0004: Action-Driven Operations

Version: 1.0  
Status: Approved

## Context

Operational platforms often become information displays.

They show records, documents, statuses and notes, but do not clearly guide the user toward the next useful action.

Complaint Managers need more than information. They need operational clarity.

They need to know what matters today.

## Decision

QCMS Operations™ will be designed around action-driven operations.

The platform should prioritise what needs attention now rather than merely displaying information.

## Reasoning

Complaint work involves many moving parts:

- Evidence requests
- Client replies
- Missing documents
- Draft complaints
- Internal notes
- Deadlines
- Submitted complaints
- Responses from providers
- Cases ready to complete

If all information is displayed equally, the most important action can become hidden.

Action-driven operations ensure Complaint Managers can quickly identify where attention is needed.

## Operational Concepts

QCMS Operations™ should support:

- Today's Mission™
- Immediate Action Required
- Waiting On Others
- Ready To Complete Today
- Operations Centre™
- Case Workspace™
- Complaint Journey™
- Workspace Actions™

These concepts help staff move from passive review to active case progression.

## Long-Term Impact

This decision ensures QCMS Operations™ remains practical, focused and useful.

The system should always answer:

- What needs attention now?
- What is waiting on someone else?
- What can be completed today?
- What is blocked?
- What should happen next?

Operational design should reduce uncertainty, not add to it.
