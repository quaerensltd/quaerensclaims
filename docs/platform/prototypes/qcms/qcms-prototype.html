Signature cancelled

Browser closed

The Platform User can reopen the flow and review any locally saved information.

Transfer failed

The workflow should pause and explain that the transfer must be retried before any CRM case is created.

CRM unavailable

The workflow should hold the completed instruction securely until the CRM handover is available.

Authority withdrawn

The instruction stops unless authority is granted again.

Consent revoked

The instruction stops and no further processing should continue unless consent is renewed.

Incomplete agreement

The Platform User must review and accept the Service Agreement before progressing.

Privacy gate

Nothing is transferred until authority is accepted, the agreement is accepted, the digital signature is completed and payment is confirmed. Only then may QCMS create the CRM case.
