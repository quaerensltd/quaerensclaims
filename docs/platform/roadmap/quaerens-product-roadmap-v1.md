# Quaerens Product Roadmap™ v1.0

Status: Master product roadmap
Authority: Subordinate to The Quaerens Platform Constitution™
Scope: Every current and future Quaerens product

## 1. Purpose

This document is the master roadmap for the evolution of The Quaerens Platform.

It replaces ad-hoc product development with an explicit, framework-owned sequence of work. Every future feature and product must be recorded on this roadmap before implementation begins.

Roadmap inclusion does not by itself authorise development, expenditure, release or deployment. It identifies intent, ownership, priority and dependencies so that implementation can be approved and governed deliberately.

If this roadmap conflicts with The Quaerens Platform Constitution™, the Constitution takes precedence.

## 3. Roadmap data standard

Every product entry must identify:

- **Name**;
- **Owning Framework**;
- **Priority**;
- **Commercial Value**;
- **Commercial Model**;
- **Current Status**;
- **Dependencies**;
- **Target Version**;
- **Estimated Development Effort**.

Permitted lifecycle status labels are:

- **Live** — released and operational;
- **In Progress** — authorised implementation is actively underway;
- **Planned** — accepted onto the roadmap but not yet in active implementation;
- **Future** — retained for later evaluation and sequencing.

Where a value has not been approved, it must be recorded as **TBD**, not guessed.

## 9. Commercial models

Every product must identify one or more approved commercial models:

| Commercial model | Definition |
|---|---|
| Free DIY | Consumer uses a self-service Framework A product without charge |
| DIY + Guided Support | Free or self-directed preparation with an optional, intentional Gateway request |
| Fixed Price | A defined service supplied for an approved fixed price |
| Professional Assessment | A paid evidence-first assessment delivered through Framework B and operational workflows |
| Consultation | A defined professional consultation journey |
| Hybrid | A documented combination of compatible models controlled by Gateway rules |

The public builder prepares the consumer's material. Framework C determines the applicable Guided Support commercial journey. Products must not embed unauthorised pricing or operational routing decisions in public pages.

## 11. Roadmap rules

- No feature may bypass its owning framework.
- No builder may duplicate framework functionality.
- No operational feature may bypass the Intake Gateway where Guided Support intake or assignment is involved.
- No CRM may receive website cases directly.
- No product may be implemented before it appears on this roadmap.
- No roadmap item may be treated as implementation approval merely because it is listed.
- No workspace may become a duplicated CRM interface.
- No category may create a standalone document, evidence, timeline, financial or routing engine where a framework already owns it.

Every new product must identify:

- Owning Framework;
- Commercial Model;
- Dependencies;
- Priority.

It must also satisfy the complete roadmap data standard before implementation begins.

## 12. Product governance and roadmap changes

Every proposed roadmap change requires:

- **Priority** — the proposed platform priority and sequencing effect;
- **Reason** — the consumer, operational or commercial need;
- **Dependencies** — frameworks, contracts, policies, data or prior releases required;
- **Framework Owner** — the single framework accountable for the capability;
- **Estimated Effort** — a proportionate implementation estimate or a recorded TBD pending discovery;
- **Target Release** — the proposed version, phase or release window.

The change process is:

1. record the proposal without beginning implementation;
2. confirm constitutional and framework ownership;
3. evaluate duplication and cross-framework effects;
4. identify commercial model and expected value;
5. define security, regression and release dependencies;
6. approve, reject or retain as a future idea;
7. version the roadmap when an approved change materially alters commitments or sequence.

Completed work must update its roadmap status and retain an auditable release reference.

## 13. Future ideas

Future ideas have a dedicated holding area so valuable concepts are not lost and are not mistaken for approved work.

Ideas are recorded.

Ideas are not development commitments.

An idea must not enter implementation until it has:

- an identified owning framework;
- a defined problem and intended value;
- an approved priority;
- a commercial model where applicable;
- known dependencies;
- an effort assessment;
- a target phase or version;
- explicit approval to move onto the product roadmap.

## 14. Roadmap maintenance

This document establishes **Quaerens Product Roadmap™ v1.0**.

The roadmap must be reviewed when:

- a product or capability is proposed;
- a dependency or framework version changes;
- work begins or completes;
- a release is verified;
- commercial or operational assumptions materially change;
- a constitutional or framework amendment affects sequencing.

Frameworks evolve independently. The roadmap records what is built next and must reference the relevant framework version without redefining that framework.

## 15. Final statement

The Quaerens Product Roadmap™ is the operational plan for the evolution of The Quaerens Platform.

Frameworks define how products are built.

The roadmap defines what is built next.

Every future product begins here before development starts.
