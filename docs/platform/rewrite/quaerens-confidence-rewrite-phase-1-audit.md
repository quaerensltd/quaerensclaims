# Quaerens Confidence Rewrite(TM) Phase 1 Audit

Version: 1.0

Status: Phase 1 Audit

Date: 1 August 2026

## Purpose

This audit records the Phase 1 public-experience pages selected for the Confidence Rewrite(TM).

The objective is not to redesign the website. The objective is to improve the first impression, emotional clarity and decision confidence of the core public journey while preserving routes, SEO intent, existing functionality, complaint builders and platform architecture.

The governing experience direction is:

Uncertainty -> Understanding -> Confidence

## Phase 1 Page Audit

| Page | Current role | Existing strength | Confidence gap | Phase 1 action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `public/index.html` | Main public entry point | Strong route coverage and useful tools | Hero is service-led rather than uncertainty-led | Reframe opening around helping visitors know what to do next |
| `public/about-quaerens.html` | Trust and company reassurance | Clear details, addresses and process information | Needs stronger human promise about confidence | Add the approved confidence promise without changing legal caution |
| `public/the-quaerens-platform.html` | Platform explanation | Strong architectural concept | Needs clearer emotional purpose | Add the "remove uncertainty" platform message |
| `public/consumer-rights-knowledge-centre.html` | Knowledge-first entry point | Broad topical guidance | Opening can better support unsure visitors | Reframe as a starting place for people who do not yet know the route |
| `public/section75support.html` | Section 75 support page | Strong search intent and legal relevance | Opens technically before reassuring the visitor | Add a plain-English confidence-led opening |
| `public/holidaypark.html` | Holiday park assessment route | Good conversion and evidence guidance | Opening can better acknowledge confusion and disappointment | Reframe hero copy around promises, documents and next steps |
| `public/foam-insulation.html` | Spray foam pillar and conversion page | Strong SEO and complaint-pack direction | Needs to keep authority while softening the first contact | Adjust hero wording only, preserving keywords and CTAs |
| `public/freeflightclaim.html` | Flight complaint pack builder | Strong self-service builder functionality | Some visible wording still suggests a narrower compensation preview | Align visible preview language with complaint-pack positioning |
| `public/energy-switch.html` | Energy complaint and switching builder | Strong builder positioning | Opening can make complex billing/switching issues feel easier to start | Reframe hero copy and CTA with confidence language |
| `public/car-finance.html` | Car finance complaint pack builder | Strong free pack builder | Opening can reduce overwhelm and clarify the user stays in control | Refine hero/support copy only |
| `public/airbnb-refunds.html` | Airbnb refund support page | Good CTR-oriented search intent | Needs more confidence-first wording around evidence and next steps | Add concise reassurance while preserving SEO phrases |

## Copy Principles For Phase 1

- Speak first to the user's uncertainty, not the organisation's process.
- Keep SEO terms visible where they already support search intent.
- Avoid promises of outcomes, guaranteed compensation or automatic submission.
- Keep the visitor in control: understand, organise, choose, review, submit.
- Preserve all existing builders, calculations, form behaviour, routes and exports.
- Make commercial routes feel clear without pressuring visitors into paid support.

## Scope Boundaries

This phase does not include:

- redesigning page layouts;
- changing canonical URLs or page slugs;
- modifying QCMS, QCMS Operations or Operations CRM;
- changing builder calculations or export architecture;
- deploying to production.
