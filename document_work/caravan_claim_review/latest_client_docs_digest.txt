The consequences of this dispute did not end with my immediate family. Over the years, the caravan had become a place where family and friends gathered to spend time together. The Human Impact of the Dispute
Draft Personal Impact Statement
Introduction
I make this statement to explain not only what happened, but what the dispute has meant to me and my family. The documents in the litigation bundle show dates, emails and records. They cannot, on their own, convey the years of uncertainty, frustration and emotional strain that accompanied this dispute.
Why We Bought the Caravan
Our family purchased the caravan believing it would provide a place to spend time together and enjoy some peace after difficult personal circumstances. We placed our trust in Haven Holidays and expected to receive what had been represented to us.
The Upgrade and the Promises
Before taking possession of the replacement caravan, numerous defects were identified. I say we were assured that the outstanding snagging items would be completed and that the caravan would be brought up to the promised standard. We relied on those assurances when deciding to proceed.
The Reality
Instead of enjoying the caravan, we found ourselves repeatedly reporting defects, arranging visits, chasing repairs and corresponding with Haven. Time that should have been spent with family became consumed by complaints, inspections and waiting for promises to be fulfilled.
Years of Chasing Repairs
The snagging process continued over an extended period. Although some work was carried out, my position is that a substantial number of defects remained unresolved. Even when we eventually returned to remove our belongings, I believe well over sixty per cent of the original snagging items had still not been satisfactorily completed.
The Pressure
I felt under constant pressure to make important decisions with incomplete information while continuing to trust assurances that matters would be put right. Every further delay increased my frustration and my sense that the issues were not being properly addressed.
Effect on My Family
This dispute affected more than finances. It affected our enjoyment of something we had hoped would bring our family together. Instead, it became a source of stress, disappointment and worry that lasted for years.
Why I Have Brought This Claim
I did not begin with the intention of taking legal action. I repeatedly tried to resolve matters directly. Only after those efforts failed, and after obtaining documentation through the Subject Access Request process, did I conclude that legal advice was necessary.
Conclusion
My aim is not simply to recount a list of defects. I want the Court and my legal advisers to understand the human impact of what happened, why I say I relied on the assurances that were given, and why I believe this dispute should now be resolved fairly on the basis of the evidence.
