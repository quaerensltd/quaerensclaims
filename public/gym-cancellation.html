Frequently Asked Questions

Gym Cancellation and Refund Questions

Can I cancel a gym membership early?

It depends on the contract, joining method, minimum term, cancellation policy and your reason for cancelling. Illness, relocation, hardship, price increases or major facility changes may need a different evidence-led approach.

Can a gym keep charging after I cancel?

Charges after cancellation should be checked against the cancellation request, notice period, payment method and the gym's response. Keep dates, bank statements and proof of cancellation.

Can I get a refund for unused membership?

Possibly, depending on the terms, what was promised, the reason the service could not be used and whether the gym had a fair basis to keep the money.

What if I joined online?

Online joining can make cooling-off and digital terms relevant. Keep the sign-up confirmation, terms shown at checkout and any cancellation instructions.

What if I cancelled my direct debit?

Cancelling a payment method does not always end the contract. If arrears or debt collection are involved, the safer route is to set out the evidence and contract position clearly.

Can illness or injury help me cancel?

It can be relevant if the contract has a medical clause or if continuing the membership is unfair in the circumstances. Medical evidence and dates usually matter.

Can moving home help me cancel?

Relocation may be relevant if the contract includes a relocation clause or the gym is no longer reasonably accessible. Keep proof of the new address and travel distance.

What if facilities changed or closed?

Service changes, closures or reduced facilities may support a complaint if the change affects what you paid for. Photos, notices, app messages and dates help.

What if a debt collector contacts me?

Do not ignore debt collection letters. Record the claimed amount, dates and documents. If formal legal documents are received, strict deadlines may apply.

Does Quaerens submit the complaint?

No. This free self-service builder creates documents for you to review and send yourself. Quaerens does not submit the complaint through this tool.
