Evidence

What Evidence Should I Keep?

Journey records

Booking confirmation, flight number, boarding pass, itinerary, ticket number and final-destination arrival time.

Airline messages

Delay, cancellation, rerouting, app, email and gate announcements, plus any written reason given by the airline.

Expense records

Meal, hotel, transport, replacement travel and communication receipts where the expense was necessary and documented.

Complaint history

Complaint reference, airline replies, rejection letters, screenshots and any escalation correspondence.

Guidance

Useful Flight Disruption Guidance

What Counts as a Flight Delay?

The most important timing question is usually the arrival time at the final destination, not only the departure delay.

Flight Cancellation Rights

Cancellations may involve refund, rerouting, care, assistance and compensation questions depending on notice, route, cause and evidence.

Missed Connections

Booking structure matters. One-booking connections should be described differently from separately booked journeys.

Denied Boarding

Voluntary and involuntary denied boarding should not be described in the same way.

Meals, Hotels and Care

Care and assistance should be recorded factually, including what was offered and what the passenger paid for.

Extraordinary Circumstances

The airline explanation should be checked carefully. This builder asks for clarification rather than deciding entitlement.

UK261 and EU261 Explained

The pack uses neutral wording about passenger-rights rules and asks the airline to consider the journey under the applicable rules.

What Happens After I Complain?

Save proof of sending, track the response, compare the airline explanation with the information requested, and check current official escalation routes.
