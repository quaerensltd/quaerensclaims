Compensation Estimate

How Much Flight Compensation Could Apply?

Under UK261 or EU261 style passenger-rights rules, compensation often depends on the final-destination arrival delay, flight distance, route, operating airline and the reason for the disruption. A cancellation may also depend on when you were told about it and what rerouting was offered.

The builder uses the information you enter to show an estimated statutory compensation figure where the route, delay and passenger count support one. It remains an estimate only because airlines may rely on legal defences such as extraordinary circumstances.

Care, Assistance and Extra Costs

Even where a flight does not clearly qualify for statutory compensation, passengers may still need to record care and expense issues. Depending on the disruption and waiting time, this can include meals or refreshments, reasonable communication costs, hotel accommodation, transport between the airport and hotel, rerouting, refund options and other documented expenses.

Guidance

Useful Flight Disruption Guidance

What Qualifies as a Flight Delay?

The timing question usually focuses on arrival at the final destination, not only when the aircraft left the gate. A flight that departs late but arrives less than three hours late may be treated differently from one that reaches the final destination three hours or more after the scheduled arrival time. The builder records scheduled and actual times so the calculation is transparent.

How Compensation Is Calculated

The estimate uses route distance, possible UK261 or EU261 scope, arrival delay and passenger count. It does not decide liability. The airline may still investigate the cause, route, operating carrier and whether any exceptional circumstances apply.

Cancelled Flights and Notice

Cancellation complaints often depend on when the airline notified you, whether rerouting was offered, whether the new arrival time was acceptable and whether expenses were caused by the cancellation. Record the notification method and keep airline messages.

Missed Connections

Missed-connection wording should explain whether the flights were booked together, which flight caused the problem and when you reached the final destination. Separate tickets may require a different explanation from one through-booked journey.

Denied Boarding

Denied boarding can involve overbooking, check-in timing, travel-document checks, voluntary surrender of a seat and rerouting. The builder keeps these facts separate so the complaint does not overstate the position.

Meals, Hotels and Care

Care and assistance can be relevant before a statutory compensation threshold is reached. Keep receipts, hotel confirmations, taxi records and screenshots showing what the airline offered or refused.

Extraordinary Circumstances

If an airline says weather, air traffic control, security, industrial action or another event caused the disruption, ask for a clear explanation and supporting evidence. The builder does not decide whether the defence is valid.

Technical, Crew or Operational Reasons

Short explanations such as “technical issue” or “operational reasons” can be too vague. The pack asks the airline to explain the actual reason and how it affected your flight.

UK261 and EU261 Scope

The possible regulation depends on the departure airport, arrival airport and operating airline. The builder uses airport and airline data where available, but passengers should still check current official guidance before sending or escalating.

Expenses and Reimbursement

Expense claims should be separated from statutory compensation. Record the amount, currency, reason, location, receipt status and evidence reference so the airline can understand what you are asking it to review.

If the Airline Rejects the Complaint

Keep the rejection letter, compare it with the evidence, ask for missing reasoning where needed and check the airline's published complaint and ADR route. Do not delete original records after a rejection.

ADR and Escalation

Escalation routes vary by airline and country. The pack includes official resources and a contact record so you can track references, dates and responses before deciding what to do next.
