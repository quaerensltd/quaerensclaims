Car finance topics explained

          

            
Understanding PCP
Personal Contract Purchase often includes monthly payments, mileage terms, optional final payment and vehicle condition rules. Keep the agreement and any explanation of the final payment.

            
Understanding HP
Hire Purchase usually spreads the purchase price over time and ownership may transfer after final payment. Keep statements, default notices and any settlement figures.

            
What is motor-finance commission?
Commission can involve payments from a lender to a dealer or broker. The relevant question is what was disclosed, how it was calculated and what records are held.

            
Discretionary commission arrangements
A discretionary commission arrangement may have allowed the broker or dealer to influence the interest rate. The generated pack asks for records without stating that wrongdoing is proven.

            
High commission arrangements
If you are concerned commission was high or unclear, the pack can ask the lender to explain what was paid and what disclosures were made.

            
What if I no longer have the agreement?
You can still create an information request. The pack can ask for agreement records, commission records and other documents held by the lender.

            
What if I do not know the lender?
The tool can create a dealer or broker request asking them to identify the lender and provide agreement details.

            
What happens after sending?
Keep proof of sending, any acknowledgement, complaint reference and future replies. Avoid duplicate complaints and check official FCA and Ombudsman updates.
