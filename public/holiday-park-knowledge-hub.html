Documents consumers should retain

Keeping the right documents can make a holiday park review clearer and faster. If you do not have everything, you can still contact us, but the following records are often useful.

  • Purchase agreement and order form
  • Park licence agreement and site rules
  • Finance agreement and payment statements
  • Sales brochures, adverts and screenshots
  • Site fee invoices and increase notices
  • Repair records, photographs and inspection reports
  • Resale, buyback or exit correspondence
  • Complaint letters, final responses and call notes
