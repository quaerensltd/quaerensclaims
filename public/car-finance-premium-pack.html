Which Option Should I Choose?

Choose the free tool if

You mainly need a personalised initial complaint letter, lender details, a basic evidence checklist and download options so you can send the complaint yourself.

Choose premium if

You want extra trackers, follow-up wording, document organisers and worksheets while still managing the complaint yourself.

Important

Buying the Premium DIY Complaint Pack does not improve eligibility, guarantee compensation or change how the lender must consider the complaint.

Digital self-help only: the Premium DIY Complaint Pack is not representation, claims management, legal advice, financial advice, a complaint-submission service or a compensation assessment.

What the Premium DIY Complaint Pack Is Intended to Include

1. Evidence organiser

Finance agreement, statements, dealer emails, lender responses and complaint history checklist.

2. Timeline tracker

A structured worksheet for dates, events, responses and document references.

3. Follow-up templates

First-person wording for acknowledgement delays, missing records and unclear responses.

4. Lender-response tracker

A simple record of contact, references, deadlines, next actions and attachments sent.

5. Final-response worksheet

A factual worksheet to compare what was asked, what was answered and what remains unclear.

6. Submission records

Email and postal cover templates plus proof-of-sending notes for your own records.
