FAQ

Platform FAQs

What is a Quaerens Complaint Pack?

A self-service document pack for organising complaint wording, evidence, timelines and requested outcomes.

What is the Quaerens Evidence Engine?

The product name for Quaerens' evidence-first question, checklist and document-organisation approach.

Are the Complaint Packs free?

The self-service Complaint Packs shown as free can be used without a success fee or account.

Does Quaerens submit complaints for me?

No. You review and submit documents yourself unless a separate guided-support arrangement is agreed.

Does Quaerens guarantee an outcome?

No. Outcomes depend on facts, evidence, deadlines and responses received.

Where is my draft stored?

In browser-local storage on this device where available.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed for the self-service tools.

Can I download my pack?

Where supported, yes: PDF, editable, text, copy and print outputs may be available.

Can I edit the documents?

Editable downloads can be reviewed and changed before submission.

Can I create more than one Complaint Pack?

Current storage usually keeps one active draft per builder. Multiple-pack support can be expanded later.

What if my issue is not listed?

Use the closest available route or contact Quaerens for guidance.

What is the difference between free DIY and guided support?

Free DIY is self-service. Guided support is separate assistance under an agreed scope.

Is Quaerens a law firm?

Quaerens provides practical dispute support and evidence organisation. These free tools are not legal advice.

Can I use a Complaint Pack for court proceedings?

The packs are not court papers. Take specialist advice if court proceedings are being considered.

How do I delete my saved draft?

Open My Complaint Packs and delete the saved draft from this browser.
