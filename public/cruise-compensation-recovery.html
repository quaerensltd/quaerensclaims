Cruise Complaint Pack FAQs

Can I claim a fixed amount for a missed cruise port?

A missed, substituted or shortened port does not automatically create a fixed entitlement. The route depends on the booking terms, reason for the change, notice given, evidence and response received.

Who should I complain to about a cruise problem?

The correct route depends on who sold the booking and what the documents say. It may be the cruise line, package organiser, travel agent, insurer, card provider or finance provider.

Should I count a future cruise credit as a cash refund?

A future cruise credit or voucher should usually be recorded separately from cash refunds so the complaint pack does not double-count or overstate the financial position.

Does Quaerens submit the cruise complaint for me?

This is a self-service builder. It helps organise your complaint pack, but you decide whether to send it and you submit it yourself through the appropriate route.
