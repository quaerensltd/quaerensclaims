Spray foam guidance

Quoted high spray foam removal costs?

Removal costs can become central evidence when spray foam has affected a mortgage, sale, survey or roof condition. The key is to show why the cost arose and how it links back to the original installation or sales explanation.

Start a free spray foam review

Quick summary: A useful spray foam removal file should separate installer documents, survey evidence, roof condition evidence and quotes. This makes the financial impact easier to understand.

Keep more than one quote where possible

If you have been quoted for removal, roof repair, ventilation work or reinstatement, keep the full written quote. A second quote can help show whether the cost is reasonable and whether different contractors identify the same issue.

Match the quote to the reason for removal

A strong file explains whether removal was recommended because of mortgage refusal, restricted roof inspection, damp, timber concern, ventilation issues or sale collapse. The quote should not sit alone; it should be matched to the survey or lender concern.

Photographs should support the documents

Photos of the loft, rafters, joists, ventilation points, damp areas and any remaining foam can help explain the problem. Keep the original photo files where possible, as dates and metadata may be useful.

Useful evidence checklist

  • Spray foam installation invoice, contract and guarantee
  • Product literature or sales promises about mortgage suitability
  • Survey comments, lender refusal notes or valuation comments
  • Removal, roof repair, timber repair and ventilation quotes
  • Photographs before, during and after removal if available
  • Any complaint or response from the installer, finance provider or scheme provider
Do not throw away old paperwork because it looks minor. Short emails, texts, brochures and grant documents can help show what you were told at the time.
